## 0.19.1

Two bugs from gate 18 in real Foundry.

- **Bug 1: a Hub action's status line and focus.** Reproduced first, on a strict fake that now delivers Foundry's update echo the way the real one does (`enableEcho`: the player's own copy of the actor changes in a later task than the relayed write, and `updateActor` fires then). Proven causes: (a) **Start researching** asked for focus on the new project's card, but the draw that followed the handler still had the OLD data (no such card yet), so the request was used up on that draw and the later echo draw had nothing to focus (focus ended on the window, failing at echo delays of 1, 30 and 400 ms); (b) the window said nothing while the relay was still answering: the echo (and the strip) update BEFORE the reply sets the result line, so the line was blank for the length of the round trip (failing test with a 400 ms reply). Not reproduced: the "queued" line being cleared by the echo of its own write (it is not, at any echo delay; a mutation that does clear it is caught) and Put aside losing focus to the page body (a mutation-safe fix is in anyway, see below). Fixes: every Hub action now sets **"Working on it..."** at once and the result when the reply arrives; focus requests (`#requestFocus`) wait for the first draw that has their control (up to `HUB.focusWaitMs`, 3 s), are cancelled by the person's own click or Tab, and Put aside now asks for the window itself (`window`) after it succeeds. The line still clears when something ELSE changes the queue (the GM resolving or rejecting it).
- **Bug 2: a rejected Compendium share was announced in PUBLIC chat** ("the GM returned Share: Gate Wolf"), from the GM queue window's Reject (chat on by default). A share rejection is now whispered to the character's owners and the GM ("... did not share "X" with the party. It stays private."), never public. Audit of the other queue chat paths: see the report; only share changed.
- Gate 18: Steps 5, 8, 9, 10 and 17 wait for the result line; Step 10 checks a second follow of the same lead is refused in plain words; Step 16 has a preflight and accepts a curly apostrophe; Step 17 says to share only Gate Ore and Gate Star; Step 18 says to use the code, not the window; new Steps 20 to 22 (share the wolf, the GM rejects it in the queue window, nothing public); the old 20 to 22 are now 23 to 25; CLEANUP also drops `gate-wolf` and `g6-field` from the shared Compendium (`sharedLeft` counts materials, experiments, creatures and regions).
- Tests: new `tests/hub-echo.test.js` (17: four flows at three echo delays, the GM-resolves case, the slow-reply case), `tests/queue-chat.test.js` (2), `tests/hub-wiring.test.js` (the screen-reader test now expects the two lines "Working on it..." and the result, each heard once), `tests/gate18.test.js`.

## 0.18.3

Three findings from gate 17 in real Foundry (Steps 22, 37, 38).

- **Keyboard did nothing in the Hub (Step 38).** Foundry's KeyboardManager cancels Tab (its "cycle view" binding) before the browser can move focus, and Space then scrolled the page. The Hub root now has one `keydown` listener (added once per frame, removed on close) that stops Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and Space from bubbling out of the Hub, only for events that came from inside it, and never calls `preventDefault` (the browser still moves focus and presses the button). Escape, arrows and every other key are untouched. The root has `tabindex="-1"` (a click on blank space focuses the window, not the page body), and on first open focus goes to the root, so the first Tab lands on Display and no ring appears by itself. A live redraw keeps the focus ring on the same button, never takes focus that was elsewhere, and falls back to the root (not the page body) when the focused control is gone. The strict test fake cannot reproduce Foundry's own handler: the proof is the gate. Rule for any future window: `docs/08`, UI surfaces.
- **"SELECTED" and button labels broke letter by letter in a narrow window (Step 37).** Badges, pills and counts never wrap or split (`white-space: nowrap`, `min-width: max-content`); a badge that does not fit beside its label drops onto its own line. The default wrapping is now `overflow-wrap: break-word` (the `anywhere` value let flex and grid items shrink to slivers, which is what broke "Dark" into "Da/rk"): text wraps at spaces and only a single word wider than its whole container breaks. Buttons in a row no longer shrink (the row wraps), and in the narrow layout the tier sits under the name and the nested padding is tighter. The window has a minimum width of 300px (`.pfp-hub-window { min-width }`, equal to `HUB.minSize.width`, a test checks; ApplicationV2 has no min-size option and clamps to the CSS min-width). Harness: 220px added to the widths (below the minimum it is a stress test: badge, overlap, position and target checks only), a check that no word of 12 characters or fewer is broken across lines at 280px and above, and that no badge is ever split; mutation-checked.
- **A stale "Craft queued" line (Step 22).** The visible status line is transient: it goes when its action leaves the queue (resolved, cancelled, gone: once it has been seen there, since the write can arrive a moment after the submit returns), when you switch character, and after `HUB.statusMs` (10 seconds). Only the current line is shown. The aria-live region only changes when the text does, so each message is announced once and clearing is silent.
- Tests: 938 in all (6 new in `hub-wiring`, 1 in `hub-layout`). Mutation checks: keyboard (no stopPropagation, preventDefault added, Escape stopped, listener stacking, not removed on close, no tabindex, no initial focus), focus (a redraw that steals focus, no fallback), status (not reconciled, no timeout, cleared too early, re-announced on every draw), and the layout (a badge that wraps letters, words broken mid-word).
- Docs: `docs/17` Steps 22, 37, 38 and the version; `docs/08` (UI surfaces) and `CLAUDE.md`: the keyboard rule.
- No manifest change except the version: no server restart; force-reload (Ctrl+Shift+R).

## 0.18.2

Layout fixes from gate 17 in real Foundry (v14.369, PF2e 8.6.1, Dark colours), and a render harness that is hostile enough to catch them before anyone clicks.

- **What was wrong.** Every clickable card is a real `<button>`, and Foundry styles plain buttons (a fixed height, centred nowrap text, a tight line height). The Formulae, material and workshop cards were squashed: second and third lines (tier, stock, "You haven't tried this here yet", an effect) printed over the next card, the Cheapest and Selected badges were hidden under the next card, names were centred, "Progress 1 of 8" had the bar through it. In a 320px window the tab row and "Workshops here: ..." were cut off at the right. The window frame's title did not follow Switch character.
- **Stylesheet (`styles/hub.css`, rewritten).** A scoped hard reset inside the Hub root: every element that decides box size gets an explicit value, and on buttons the properties that can squash a card (height, line height, white space, overflow, text alignment, display) are `!important`, so no Foundry rule can win whatever its specificity. A card is a grid of rows (name and tier, stock, effect, badges) that GROWS to fit; the 44px minimum is a `min-height`, never a height. Badges, pills and the progress bar sit in the card's flow (nothing is positioned absolutely). Everything wraps; the status strip, tabs and panels reflow down to 280px at every text size; in a narrow window the tier goes under the name. The option card's effect list is spans now (a `<ul>` inside a `<button>` was invalid HTML). Targets still 44px (`HUB.minTarget`).
- **Frame title.** `showCharacter` now asks the frame for a new title (the documented `window.title` render option) and `_onRender` keeps the title element in step on every draw.
- **Default colours.** Nothing in the code or the stylesheet reads `prefers-color-scheme` or Foundry's theme classes: the theme is the `data-theme` the window sets from the player's own saved Display choice, and anything unknown falls back to Parchment (tested). A window that opened Parchment and later Dark means a Dark choice was saved earlier in that browser (it is a per-browser setting); Display, Reset to defaults puts Parchment back.
- **The harness** (`build/harness.mjs`, run by `build/layout-check.mjs` and `tests/hub-layout.test.js`). It draws every Hub state with the real render function and the real stylesheet, inside a window that imitates Foundry around it, at three hostile strengths (Foundry's button, input, heading, paragraph, list, fieldset and legend rules at 2rem and 2.5rem, overflow hidden and visible, plain, high specificity and `!important`) and without them; in all three themes at 100% and 175% (and 125% and 150% in the narrow windows), at 900, 600, 320 and 280px: 1,040 renders. Per render it checks, with real geometry: no element's content spills out of its box, no two siblings overlap, nothing is positioned absolutely, every control is at least 44px, nothing runs past the right edge or makes the window scroll sideways, no text is clipped by an ancestor that hides overflow. A mutation test re-adds a fixed card height, an absolute badge, a non-wrapping tab row, small targets and nowrap text and requires every one to fail. The tests are skipped (with a message) where Chromium is not installed, as on the CI runner, unless `REQUIRE_CHROMIUM=1`.
- Screenshots: `docs/screens/` redrawn inside the hostile window (`node build/screens.mjs`).
- Tests: 931 in all (5 new: four layout tests and the frame-title test). No manifest change except the version: no server restart; force-reload (Ctrl+Shift+R) to drop the cached stylesheet.

## 0.18.1

Fix: the Downtime Hub did not open in real Foundry (gate 17 Step 4). `TypeError: Cannot set property state of #<ApplicationV2> which has only a getter`.

- **Root cause.** `HubWindow` kept its own window state in `this.state`, but `ApplicationV2.state` is a read-only getter (its render lifecycle: NONE, RENDERING, RENDERED, CLOSING, CLOSED), so the constructor threw. The first fake `ApplicationV2` in the tests had no such getter, so every test passed. The window's own state is now `this.hubState` (a name nothing in Foundry uses).
- **Audit.** Every name `HubWindow` and the review window define or assign was checked against the v14.369 `ApplicationV2` declarations (the community `foundry-vtt-types` package for 14.369; foundryvtt.com itself is not reachable from the build sandbox). `state` was the only collision. The scene-controls tool, the sheet header control (`onClick` on a header entry is in the v14 types), `DEFAULT_OPTIONS`, `actions` binding, `_renderHTML`/`_replaceHTML`/`_onRender`/`_onPosition`/`_onClose`, `render({ force: true })` and `foundry.applications.instances` match the declarations.
- **A stricter fake.** `tests/fake-application.js` mirrors the real class: read-only getters (state, id, element, rendered, title, window, classList, form, minimized, hasFrame, children, parent) that throw on assignment, a frozen `options`, the real render lifecycle and its order (a plain `render()` never opens a closed window), clicks on `[data-action]` routed to `actions` with the real `this` and arguments, and a real DOM (jsdom, a new dev dependency). Both the Hub tests and the review-window tests now run on it (the review test used to assign `app.element`, which the real class also forbids). A collision check compares what the windows define with the full member list of the real class, and a static test refuses any assignment to a read-only member.
- Tests: 926 in all (6 new). `tests/hub-wiring.test.js` now renders the Hub for real and clicks every action in `actions` through the DOM.
- No manifest change (the version only): a force-reload of both windows is enough if you already restarted for 0.18.0.

## 0.17.0

Hidden difficulty everywhere, and the Experiments knowledge system (Stage 10d; the decisions are in `docs/11b-hub-addendum.md`).

- **Players never see a DC, odds, a percentage or difficulty wording.** Fixed at the source: the explore and research rows of `api.queue.list()` (a player's row is plain; the GM's keeps the DC and the odds), `api.projectInfo`, the research summary (now a whisper with degree words; the total and DC go in a GM-only whisper), the project a relayed upgrade or design start returns, `api.audit.*` (GM-only), and the player copy of definitions (a region loses its DC and spawn table; a material its effects and loss chance; a recipe its overrides, outputs, craft level, research tier and loss chances, and gains precomputed craft `days`). A formula's baseline (what the cheapest valid materials make) is revealed when it is owned (research, a GM grant, a commission that teaches); a loan does not reveal it. A loaned formula teaches the crafter (`EXPERIMENTS.ownerLearnsFromLoan`, default false). New share kind `experiment`. `api.compendium.view` gains `entries.experiments`; new `api.compendium.knownEffect` and `revealBaselines` (GM: for formulas gained before this version).
- Existing formula snapshots are rewritten without `craftLevel` the first time a GM loads; the GM sees one "refreshed N formula copy(ies)" notice.
- Tests: 859 in all (46 new): the return-value scanner (`tests/difficulty.test.js`), Experiments (pure and wired), and the two gates. ## 0.16.0

Players load only the definitions they need.

- A player client no longer reads any definition pack. Both packs (recipes and the rest) are hidden from players' interface (`module.json` pack ownership; **restart the Foundry server once**), and the GM client sends each player the definitions their characters need over a new guarded socket handler, `definitionsFor`: recipes they hold a formula for (own and loaned), blueprints they hold or are designing, the materials they have, the stations and towns in play, the regions tagged on their scene and the node types out there. Honor system, not secrecy.
- Stripped from the player copy: a region's spawn table, a node type's ladders and charges. A region's DC is kept on purpose (`api.queue.list()` already shows it to players; see `docs/10`).

- Fixed in a player window: previewing a recipe the character holds said "unknown recipe"; exploring a region in play said `unknown region`. A recipe the character does not hold now says "`<name>` does not know this formula."; "orphaned formula" is said only when a held formula has no recipe on the GM; a material the character has never met is "you do not have ...", not "unknown".
- Tests: 34 new (pure selection and stripping, per-client wiring, the two gates). The fake Foundry can give every client its own definitions (off by default). The 10a/10b/10c suites run as before; only the handler counts (6 handlers now), the "a player skips the recipes pack" rule and the definitions-pack ownership guard changed with the rule.
